# Verity concept preview

## Status

Exploratory wireframe requested on 13 September 2026. These are provisional design choices, not a locked product specification. The original extension and supplied DOCX are preserved. This preview is a separate, dependency-free browser artifact in `wireframe/`.

## Register

product

## Users

Students studying class materials. A Singapore secondary-school sample profile is a design assumption suggested by the handwritten exam references, not a confirmed target age. Desktop study is the primary preview; navigation and panels also adapt to narrow screens.

## Product Purpose

Make class materials easier to understand through a connected tutor and whiteboard, then let students practise and inspect evidence of their progress. Opportunities and portfolio explore the longer-term concept in the supplied photos.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, calm, encouraging. The Verity name and gold mascot come from the existing extension. Concise guidance without claiming the tutor knows everything.

## Anti-references

Provisional design guardrails: avoid competitive student leaderboards, shame-based streaks, opaque intelligence scores, career predictions from grades, or a crowded chat-only interface.

## Design Principles

- One clear next action, with the full proposed feature set reachable.
- Class material remains visible beside learning activities.
- Hints lead to an independent attempt; progress shows the evidence behind a number.
- Opportunities invite exploration rather than predicting a student's future.
- Distinguish original features, proposed improvements, and unimplemented services.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Design defaults, not a verified compliance claim: keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, semantic navigation and dialogs, readable contrast, text alternatives for charts, reduced-motion handling, responsive layouts. No sound or animated mascot is required to learn.

## Source boundaries

- DOCX: Google Classroom material context, subject workspace, tutor and whiteboard, generated quizzes, stats, exam entry, topic retries.
- Photo 1: sequential shapes, tutor hints, optional image-generation fallback.
- Photo 2: subject tracking, career/opportunity discovery, portfolio and event records.
- Existing extension: Verity name, launcher, idle/loading GIFs and static gold mascot.
- The team supplied Challenge 4 in chat: Equity & Access Across All Pathways. It addresses unequal access to internships, industry exposure, career guidance and enrichment, structural exclusion by institution/pathway/background, recognition through capability and character, and support beyond class. No challenge statement was present in the DOCX itself.
- Challenge-focused additions (access filters, support preferences/checklist, student-controlled evidence sharing) are proposals. They do not replace the original learning-first concept without team agreement. An access-first product is the provisional recommendation for closer challenge alignment.
- The DOCX conflicts between 50% quiz / 50% exam and 70% quiz / 30% exam. The preview defaults to separate evidence and provides both formulas as explicit comparison modes in Progress.
- All student records, learning material extracts, tutor replies, activities and opportunity listings in the preview are invented demo content, not collected data or live recommendations.
